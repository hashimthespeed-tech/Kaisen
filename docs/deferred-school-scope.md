# Deferred school enrollment and verification

This is a parking place for institutional school features that the owner explicitly removed from the active Accrete app specification. Do not build or ask about these while resolving the current app questions. Revisit them only when the owner returns to school enrollment.

## Previously discussed direction

- The United States is the first intended country, and students are age 14 or older in the current app plan.
- Teacher signup is open in the current app plan.
- For an eventual school rollout, a teacher would be verified as working under a school that has signed up for Accrete.
- In that eventual school-based signup, students would select their school and grade instead of supplying a date of birth.

## Deferred gaps

- How schools enroll, who administers them, and how teacher affiliation is verified.
- When open teacher signup would end and whether a teacher could be attached to multiple schools.
- How school and grade would be verified and how the age-14 minimum would be established without date of birth.
- School-specific privacy, contracting, and student-data requirements.

These are future institutional questions, not requirements in the current [app specification](app-spec.md).
