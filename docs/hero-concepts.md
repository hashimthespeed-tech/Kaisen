# Three independent Reign hero concepts

The owner asked to build all three proposed directions separately and choose afterward. This feature branches from the current unapproved motion preview; main remains untouched. A comparison page links to three full-width routes, with no concept selected for production.

Forest #072724, surface #0f3933, mint #97fcd7, paper #f5f8f7, muted #526d65. Playfair display / Inter controls remain shared. Each design moves the illustrative portfolio below the hero and preserves working teacher setup.

A — Cinematic sculpture: centered editorial typography over an original, luminous emerald ribbon sculpture. A slow three-dimensional rotation and deliberate camera angle changes. Composition is a single art object, not a dashboard card.
B — Interactive decision: full-width stage with moving abstract paths; the visitor pauses time, chooses a direction, then releases it. This demonstrates agency without inventing investment teaching or evaluating a financial answer. Its status explicitly identifies an interaction study, not a lesson.
C — Miniature world: a small architectural landscape of fictional businesses. Selecting a business illuminates its connections and presents a short fictional role, not market research or advice. More playful and tactile; no financial predictions or real company facts.

Build original Three.js geometry with procedural studio lighting, no borrowed third-party art or textures. Animate with one render loop per mounted scene; pause offscreen/hidden/reduced motion. Provide keyboard/tap controls and a static fallback if WebGL fails. Desktop/mobile validation and an offline ZIP make comparison reviewable. The prior Awwwards research remains blocked and is not a claimed basis for these designs.

Each file embeds the application, fonts, and Three.js; no external textures or network services are needed. License notices and desktop reference screenshots are included. TypeScript, 9 model/storage tests, the original teacher walkthrough, and the concept browser checks passed. The concept checks cover all three rendered scenes, both decision paths, business selection, camera buttons, pause/resume, reduced motion, WebGL failure, offline assets, and 320/390/768/1440px layouts. Browser checks used Chromium with software WebGL in the cloud; this is not a claim of measured frame rates on physical school devices.

Three.js adds roughly 140 KiB gzip versus the prior application's bundle; the comparison build intentionally includes all alternatives in one offline package. Once a direction is selected, unused concepts should be removed and production delivery can split the 3D code. No real curriculum or financial outcomes have been added.
