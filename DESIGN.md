# MOMENT landing direction

MOMENT is the provisional name for the classroom investing experience. The landing page starts with the owner's selected night observatory illustration. Two matching painted continuations take the view down the path, across a cutaway rock face, and into a restrained crystal cavern. `scripts/build-moment-mural.py` blends the source paintings from `artwork/moment/`, reconstructs the mural with an FSRCNN 4× model, and cuts four pieces from each painting: twelve adjacent 3840px-wide WebP tiles. They must render as one continuous vertical mural, with no background switch, independent motion, or visible seam. The original 941px paintings remain the source; the 4K tiles are learned upscales, not newly painted 4K originals.

The landing page follows the hero and small interactive product preview with a reserved film space, an editorial statement about looking beyond price, an inquiry scene, a teacher setup preview, a practice scene, and a brief closing invitation. The inquiry and practice scenes occupy the long scenic gaps as single liquid-glass surfaces with useful product questions, not decorative card stacks. The film remains a liquid-glass placeholder until the inside of the product is built; once there is real footage, it will autoplay muted and inline with an accessible pause control. The clouds painted into the chosen image stay fixed in that composition. The separate cloud cutout had a visible rectangular edge and has been removed. The earlier telescope and three-card sequence are rejected and are absent from this review.

Use midnight navy, moonlight white, and restrained warm amber for the landing presentation. Keep headline copy short, consistently aligned, and in title case; keep the product preview readable and the background visible around both. On mobile, crop the same mural toward the moon, observatory, and crystal detail. Product values and decisions are illustrative and virtual.

Instrument Sans Variable is the landing display face. It brings a firmer, more assured shape to the name and headlines while the working prototype keeps its existing UI type. The teacher preview describes browser-local setup truthfully; joining, market data, and trading controls are not presented as live.

On entry, the night scene clears from a brief dark veil while the headline resolves into focus and the preview settles in. Research choices transition their evidence; the glass film, inquiry, teacher fields, and practice scene reveal as distinct moments during the descent. Glass surfaces drift a few pixels in response to scrolling, while the mural and its painted clouds never move. Reduce Motion removes spatial motion and the glass glint.

Future 3D feature demonstrations belong in a separate visual space from the mural. They must show actual MOMENT product experiences, such as researching a company, making a virtual situation decision, or reviewing a portfolio. Build them from consistent, high-resolution pre-rendered frames with a still fallback. The page should only play the sequence and manage its visibility; CSS chart drawing, card stacks, ornamental lenses, and background parallax are not the feature animations. Do not invent lessons, market facts, or scenario outcomes for these assets.

The feature storyboards and playback approach are in `docs/moment-feature-sequences.md`.
